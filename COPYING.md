# libkenwoodk

Kotlin driver for Kenwood CAT rigs: TS-890S/TS-990S control over KNS LAN or serial, including the 640-bin LAN bandscope stream.

Canonical repository: <https://github.com/nrolabs/libkenwoodk>
Maintained by Isak — PU3IAR.

Shipped inside [iSDR Drivers](https://github.com/nrolabs/isdr-drivers) as a
submodule; the byte-exact protocol tests live there, next to the other
driver suites.
